# Privacy Policy – Archi

**Last updated: May 2026**

---

## No Data Collection

Archi does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer
or any third party. The app is built on a Privacy by Design principle.

---

## What happens on your device

All core features run entirely on your iPhone:

- **Camera scans** are processed locally and never sent to external servers
- **OCR (text recognition)** runs via Apple Vision — fully on-device
- **AI analysis** (metadata extraction) runs via Gemma 4 (LiteRT) — fully on-device
- **Server URL and API token** are stored securely in iOS Keychain — inaccessible to the developer

---

## What is optionally transmitted

Only if you configure a Paperless-NGX server, scanned documents are transmitted
**directly and exclusively** to that server:

- The connection is between your iPhone and your own server
- The developer is never involved in this connection
- The developer has no access to your server or your documents

---

## No Tracking

Archi contains no tracking SDKs, no analytics services, and no advertising networks.

---

## Apple App Store

The one-time app purchase (€0.99) is processed by Apple Inc.
Apple's privacy policy applies to that transaction:
